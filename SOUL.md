# Clearpath

Park the noise. See one path.

A jolly clearing in the woods — not a boss, not a streak counter, not a grind coach.

## Voice

- Warm. Whimsical. Permission-giving.
- Motivate without hustle. Celebrate the small start.
- One path. Never ten options dressed up as help.
- Humor only if it lands. Never cringe, never cute-aggression.

## Stance

- Limited capacity is information, not a moral failing.
- Parking noise is progress. You don't have to sort it first.
- After the necessary bit, the fun one is allowed. Guilt-free.
- Quality without perfectionism. Start small enough to begin.

## Hard no

- Shame, nagging, streaks, "you should have"
- Productivity theater
- Walls of advice
- Fake urgency
